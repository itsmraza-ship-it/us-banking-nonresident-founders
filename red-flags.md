# Red Flags — Scams Targeting Non-Resident Founders

Banking is the step where founders are most vulnerable, because it's the step they understand least and need most. Here's what to watch for.

## 🚩 "Guaranteed account approval"

**No one can guarantee a bank approves you.** Approval decisions belong to the bank or fintech alone, based on their compliance and risk rules. Anyone selling "guaranteed approval" is either lying or planning to submit fraudulent documents in your name — which can get *you* blacklisted.

Legitimate help sounds like: *"we'll guide you through the application and help you prepare."* That's what Epics Deals offers. Anything stronger than that is a lie.

## 🚩 Upfront "account opening fees" paid to strangers

Real banks and fintechs don't charge a random intermediary a fee to "open" your account. If someone on Telegram, WhatsApp, or a forum asks for an upfront fee to "secure" your US bank account, walk away. The only legitimate costs are the provider's own published fees (which you pay to the provider, in the application).

## 🚩 Buying "aged" or "verified" accounts

Offers to sell you a pre-existing US bank account (or a Stripe/PayPal account) "ready to use." These accounts are tied to someone else's identity and KYC. Using one is a terms-of-service violation at best and identity fraud at worst — and the provider will freeze the funds the moment anything looks off. Your money, their name on the account: you lose.

## 🚩 Sharing your login credentials with a "helper"

No legitimate advisor needs your bank login. Anyone asking for it is either phishing or planning to operate the account as you. Real guidance happens with *you* logged in, or through official referral/introduction channels.

## 🚩 Fake "bank representatives" in DMs

Scammers impersonate bank staff on WhatsApp and Telegram, complete with logos and employee-sounding titles. Real banks don't onboard business customers over WhatsApp DMs. If someone contacts you first claiming to be from a bank, go to the bank's official site and start the process there yourself.

## 🚩 Nominee / straw-owner schemes

"Pay us and we'll put a US person as your company's owner so the bank approves you." This misrepresents beneficial ownership to a financial institution — a serious compliance violation that can unwind your company, freeze funds, and create personal liability. Don't do it.

## The safe way to get help

- Work with providers' **official** application flows and published requirements.
- Use advisors who explain the process and help you **prepare** — never ones who promise outcomes.
- Keep every document, confirmation, and correspondence. If something goes wrong, a paper trail is your best asset.
