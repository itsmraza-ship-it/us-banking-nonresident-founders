# US Banking for Non-Resident Founders

You formed the LLC. You got the EIN. Now the part everyone underestimates: **getting a US business bank account without living in the US.**

Traditional banks mostly want you (and sometimes your co-founders) to show up in person with US ID. For non-resident founders, the realistic path is **fintech banking platforms** — and choosing the wrong one, or applying unprepared, costs weeks.

This repo is an honest, hype-free map of the options, what banks actually ask for, and the scams to avoid.

- **Approval is subject to the bank's decision.** What you *can* control is showing up prepared: clean documents, a real business story, and the right platform for your situation.
